# Decision log

This is the append-only history of durable product and architecture choices. Each numbered row
records the decision, its reason, alternatives when recorded, and its owning source. “Not recorded”
means the cited source does not establish that detail; it is not a reconstructed justification.
Dates identify an explicit approval or dated design where available; a dated design does not by
itself establish the day the decision was made. The initial backfill is ordered by dated sources,
with undated decisions at the end except D18, which precedes its documented revisit.
Append new rows with the actual decision date. Never edit or renumber a historical row to reverse
it: append a row that says “Supersedes D<n>” and links the new source. For current product behavior,
use the latest applicable decision and the concise summary in [product.md](agents/product.md).
Older designs describe their time, including superseded Toggl plans in the
[final-form design](2026-07-07-intersect-final-form-design.md).
